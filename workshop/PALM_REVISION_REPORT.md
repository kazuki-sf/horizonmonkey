## What two hostile review passes changed

A first pass found ten problems; a second, after the fixes, found six more ---
**two of which the fixes themselves introduced.** Both rounds were reproduced
independently before acting.

Most serious found: §6 asserted "it did not remove the constraint" while the
project's own frozen scorer showed monotone erosion inside the tested horizon,
already computed and sitting unused in `macros.tex`. Second: §6 swapped arms
mid-sentence, taking intent from `natural` and verification from
`natural-padded`, and the arm it did not print has the opposite sign.

**Introduced by my own fix and then caught:** replacing the padded contrast with
the raw one dropped the pre-registered primary hypothesis H41 and printed a
figure 8 points larger with no length control --- turning a fix into an
overclaim. Restored. Also caught: Figure 2's legend said "quantified negative"
when the plotted series is the scorer's *qualifier stated* judgment, and the
quantified negative never falls at all (60/60 at every generation).

Other defects found along the way, unrelated to the review: the **submitted v1
PDF rendered no bibliography at all**, and seven citation keys in my first draft
did not exist in `refs.bib`.

## Unresolved reviewer objections

1. **The plan manipulation is also a topical prime.** Naming
   `promotional_pricing` makes pricing salient as well as making it the plan.
   Removing the action field defeats the narration route, not this one. Stated
   in §3 and §8; the cell that would settle it was not run.
2. **No arm anywhere states a constraint that is false or stale.** The safety
   story turns on an agent failing to check a belief whose constraint has gone
   stale, and that condition is untested. Stated in §7.
3. **Two of six models carry most of the constraint effect.** Excluding them it
   is +7.8 and +13.4. Direction transfers; magnitude does not. Stated beside the
   headline, in the figure, and in §8.
4. **The upstream and downstream halves are identified separately**, not end to
   end. Stated three times.
5. **Six generations is not twenty**, and the erosion trend is a decline
   observed inside the horizon rather than a plateau, which is a reason not to
   extrapolate the negative result. Stated in §6 and its figure.
